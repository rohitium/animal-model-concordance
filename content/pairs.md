# Dog and cat drug pairs

::: lede
{{n_pairs}} agent-and-indication pairs used both in companion animals with naturally occurring
disease and in people, classified by whether the veterinary and human evidence point the same way.
:::

{{pairs_table}}

::: small
"Discordant" is used only where all the available evidence points the opposite way; "indeterminate"
means the evidence on one side was not strong enough to call, and those pairs are kept in view
rather than dropped. Timing says whether the drug was approved in humans before or after the
veterinary evidence — most were approved first. The strata and confidence intervals are on the
[report page](index.html#pairs).
:::
